## 3. Simsbury specifics

**Permits (VERIFIED — https://www.simsbury-ct.gov/391/Building-Department, 2026-07-26):**
- Building permit fee: **$16.26 per $1,000 of construction cost**. $200K addition ≈ $3,252.
- Zoning Compliance Review Application: **$25**; Certificate of Occupancy: $5.
- Apply via CITYSQUARED or in person; requires plans + scaled plot plan. Building Dept: 860-658-3234.
- Possible sign-offs: Zoning, Wetlands, Historic District, Health (septic), Sewer, Engineering, Fire Marshal.
- Timeline: CT 30-day statutory review (https://www.jaspector.com/permits/connecticut/hartford/simsbury/ — VERIFIED); CT-wide typical 3–5 weeks (Superior Remodelers).

**Zoning setbacks (Simsbury Zoning Regulations §3.9):**
- **R-40**: front 50 ft, side 40 ft, rear 50 ft; min lot 40,000 sqft, frontage 200 ft — VERIFIED via ZBA variance notices citing §3.9 (https://www.simsbury-ct.gov/m/newsflash/Home/Detail/712, https://www.simsbury-ct.gov/m/newsflash/home/detail/539).

- Town's posted "DRAFT Zoning Template 12-18-25" has empty bulk tables — unusable. **Zone of 10 Bob White Way: UNVERIFIED** — confirm via Simsbury GIS/Assessor or https://www.zoneomics.com/zoning-maps/connecticut/simsbury.

**Septic (Farmington Valley Health District — VERIFIED, form from https://www.granby-ct.gov/DocumentCenter/View/127/FVHD-Location-Approval-Process; FVHD serves Simsbury per https://fvhd.org/ and https://www.simsbury-ct.gov/416/Farmington-Valley-Health-District):**
- Fee **$60**; approval faxed to town building dept.

- Form undated — fee may have changed; FVHD (860) 352-2333.

## 8. Living through construction (kids + dog)

Ground-level additions usually livable-through (wall stays closed until late "button-up"); second-story/kitchen work often forces temporary move-out (https://foremostbuilders.com/blog/can-i-live-in-home-during-home-addition/ ; https://www.meadowlarkbuilders.com/blog/living-through-a-renovation-to-stay-or-not-to-stay). Realities: ~7am contractor traffic for months, dust, periodic utility shutoffs, open excavation (dog containment issue), jobsite hazards for kids/pets (https://pittsburghadditions.com/can-i-live-in-my-home-with-an-addition-being-built/ ; https://www.betterbuilders.com/blog/living-on-site-during-a-home-renovation). Dog: daycare/off-site on demo days; white noise (https://www.fearfreehappyhomes.com/my-pets-survived-our-home-renovations-and-yours-can-too/). Takeout/temp-kitchen budget if kitchen touched (https://www.levelworksinc.com/post/live-home-during-renovation). All UNVERIFIED advice-content, low stakes.
