## 7. Psychology research: moving vs staying with school-age kids

- **Jelleyman & Spencer (2008)**, systematic review, J Epidemiology & Community Health: childhood residential mobility → behavioral/emotional problems (cited in https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2019.02311/full). VERIFIED-citation.
- **Oishi & Schimmack (2010)**, JPSP 98(6): more childhood moves → lower adult well-being among introverts; poorer social relationships. https://www.apa.org/pubs/journals/releases/psp-98-6-980.pdf VERIFIED.
- Norwegian registry cohort (Frontiers in Psychology 2019, above) + **Calhoun 2026, Population, Space and Place** (https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/psp.70194): school moves are the more damaging component vs residential moves. VERIFIED-citation.
- **AACAP Facts for Families #14 "Children and Family Moves"**: https://www.aacap.org/AACAP/Families_and_Youth/Facts_for_Families/FFF-Guide/Children-And-Family-Moves-014.aspx VERIFIED.
- Child Mind Institute: https://childmind.org/article/advice-for-moving-with-children/ VERIFIED URL.
- Adolescents ~4-6 months to form friendships post-move (UNVERIFIED primary).
- **Critical caveat**: the literature penalizes moves that CHANGE school/community. A within-zone or within-Simsbury move preserves middle/high continuity; same-zone move preserves everything. Renovation-stress literature not found in peer-reviewed form (do not cite as research).

1. Bob White Way is zoned for the best-testing, newest-renovated elementary in Simsbury (Latimer Lane, #36/939 CT, $39M renovation) and is ~0.2-0.3 mi walk away. Moving within town = zero school upgrade at any level.
2. Low-traffic internal loop street, 25 mph, mapped footpaths to school; sidewalks unverified.
3. Exceptional kid+dog amenities within ~5 min (Stratton Brook, Simsbury Farms, in-neighborhood trail connector, dog park 4 mi).
4. Taxes rising (34.16 → ~34.8 combined proposed); 689-unit development pending on Weatogue side — the two biggest local uncertainties.
5. Mobility research penalizes school-changing moves; within-zone moves or renovation avoid that documented cost.
