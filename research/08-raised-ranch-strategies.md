## 1. Rear main-level addition
- Main floor sits ~4 ft above grade → rear addition needs elevated foundation: (a) frost walls + tall crawlspace, (b) full foundation (near-free lower-level volume since walls must be ~8 ft anyway), (c) piers (cold floor, rarely recommended in CT). Build-out $150–330/sf vs build-up $280–560/sf (https://finehomecontracting.com/how-much-does-a-home-addition-cost-in-connecticut/, SNIPPET).

## 9. ANSI Z765 appraisal rule — CRITICAL
**VERIFIED — https://selling-guide.fanniemae.com/sel/b4-1.3-05/improvements-section-appraisal-report:**
- ANSI Z765-2021 required since Apr 2022 (Freddie aligned).
- "Fannie Mae considers a level to be below-grade if any portion of it is below-grade, regardless of the quality of its finish or the window area of any room."
- "a walk-out partially below-grade area with finished rooms would not be included in the above-grade square footage or room count." Below-grade value goes on the basement line.
**Consequence (INFERRED, high confidence): the house appraises as 1,505 sf GLA.** Finished lower level (571 sf now, ~830 sf potential) + any garage conversion = basement-line space, credited at **50–60% of above-grade $/sf** (market range 50–75%, garden-level at top of band — mymotherlode/plansnapper/mckissock, SNIPPETs; post-2022 GLA inclusion dead on agency loans: https://www.getloans.com/blog/below-grade-square-footage-gets-erased/). Only a rear addition adds GLA. But lower-level finish at ~$50/sf cost vs ~50% credit on ~$300/sf value still prices out favorably (INFERRED).
- If a bedroom/bath are on the lower level, appraisals read 3BR/2BA above grade + finished LL (UNVERIFIED which rooms are down — listing says all 4 BR up; 3rd bath down).

## 10. Over-improvement ceiling (street best comp $452.5K)

- **Applied: sensible improvement spend ≈ $50–100K** — covers CAC/heat pump ($8–15K net), lower-level finish ($30–60K), egress ($3–6K), entry refresh ($2–10K per Block VERIFIED). Excludes second story, $150K+ suite addition (16–27% recoup), garage conversion+new garage.
