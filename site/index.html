The current home

A 54×26 raised ranch: rich in bedrooms and baths, short on living space — and it has no air conditioning.

Bought August 2020 for $310K after 4 days on market; worth roughly $453–553K today. Four bedrooms and three full baths — rare at this size — but only 1,505 sq ft above grade, and the assessor records 0% cooling. The 0.92-acre lot is the hidden asset: fully conforming, public sewer, and ~53 ft of by-right room to build behind the house.

Assessed $233,030 (2022 reval) · tax $7,960/yr at 34.16 mills, ≈$8,113 at the new FY27 rate. Value estimate spans Redfin's $552.8K AVM and the street's last actual sale at $452.5K — treat the midpoint as a working number, not an appraisal.

The financial crux

The golden handcuffs are even tighter here: a probable sub-3% loan.

The family closed on August 7, 2020 — PMMS printed 2.88% that week, its all-time low at the time. If they financed at purchase and never refinanced, they hold a ~2.9–3.0% mortgage. Moving re-prices everything at today's 6.58%; improving borrows only the project at ~7.75% while the cheap first mortgage survives untouched.

A project funded by a fixed second lien (home-equity loan, ~7.36–8.08% in late July 2026) leaves the first mortgage untouched.
